# ScriptVoice Commission Dispute Resolution Rules

**Effective Date:** February 2026
**Last Updated:** February 1, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

These rules govern the resolution of disputes arising from commissioned audio performances on ScriptVoice. By using the commission feature, both Writers and Performers agree to abide by these rules.

---

## 2. Definitions

- **Writer**: The screenplay author who requests and pays for a commissioned performance
- **Performer**: The voice artist (solo or group) who accepts and delivers the commissioned work
- **Commission**: The contracted agreement for audio performance of a screenplay
- **Platform**: ScriptVoice and its designated dispute resolution team
- **Deliverable**: The audio recording submitted by the Performer

---

## 3. Scope of Disputes

### 3.1 Valid Dispute Reasons

**Writer may dispute if:**
1. Deliverable significantly deviates from the screenplay
2. Audio quality is objectively poor (severe distortion, background noise, inaudible sections)
3. Wrong characters were voiced or characters were omitted
4. Performer fails to deliver within agreed timeframe + 7-day grace period
5. Performer becomes unresponsive for 14+ days during active commission

**Performer may dispute if:**
1. Writer requests changes beyond the scope of included retakes
2. Writer is unresponsive for 14+ days after submission
3. Writer makes unreasonable demands not in original agreement
4. Writer provides feedback that cannot be reasonably implemented

### 3.2 Invalid Dispute Reasons

The following are NOT valid grounds for dispute:
- Subjective preference ("I don't like the voice")
- Style differences not specified in original request
- Changes to the screenplay after commission acceptance
- Buyer's remorse
- Price disagreements after acceptance
- Personality conflicts

---

## 4. Pre-Dispute Requirements

### 4.1 Good Faith Communication

Before opening a formal dispute, parties MUST:
1. Communicate through the platform's messaging system
2. Clearly articulate the issue
3. Propose a reasonable solution
4. Allow 72 hours for response
5. Attempt at least one revision cycle (if retakes remain)

### 4.2 Documentation

Parties should maintain:
- All communication through the platform
- Original screenplay and any approved modifications
- All submitted audio files
- Timestamps of submissions and responses

---

## 5. Dispute Process

### 5.1 Opening a Dispute

1. Navigate to the commission detail page
2. Click "Open Dispute"
3. Select dispute category
4. Provide detailed explanation (500+ characters required)
5. Upload supporting evidence if applicable
6. Submit dispute

### 5.2 Response Period

- Responding party has **72 hours** to submit their response
- Response must address all claims
- Response may include counter-evidence
- Failure to respond may result in automatic ruling against non-responding party

### 5.3 Platform Review

The Platform will:
1. Review all submitted evidence within 5 business days
2. Listen to submitted audio deliverables
3. Compare deliverable against screenplay
4. Review all platform messages
5. Consider industry standards for audio quality

### 5.4 Additional Information

The Platform may:
- Request additional documentation from either party
- Request clarification on specific points
- Consult with industry experts if needed
- Extend review period for complex cases (up to 10 additional business days)

---

## 6. Resolution Outcomes

### 6.1 Performer Upheld

**When the Performer has fulfilled their obligations:**
- Full payment released to Performer
- Commission marked as complete
- Writer may leave feedback (marked as disputed)

### 6.2 Writer Upheld

**When the Performer has not met reasonable standards:**
- Full refund issued to Writer
- Commission marked as cancelled (disputed)
- Performer may respond to any feedback

### 6.3 Partial Resolution

**In rare cases where both parties share responsibility:**
- Payment split as determined by Platform
- Typical splits: 75/25, 60/40, or 50/50
- Both parties may leave feedback

### 6.4 Mutual Cancellation

**If both parties agree to cancel:**
- Full refund to Writer (minus processing fees if applicable)
- No negative impact on either party's record
- Commission marked as mutually cancelled

---

## 7. Appeal Process

### 7.1 Eligibility

- One appeal allowed per dispute
- Must be filed within 7 calendar days of resolution
- New evidence must be presented
- Appeals on identical grounds will be rejected

### 7.2 Appeal Review

- Conducted by different reviewer than original
- Review period: up to 10 business days
- Decision is final and binding

---

## 8. Quality Standards

### 8.1 Audio Quality Minimums

Acceptable deliverables must meet these standards:
- Clear, audible speech throughout
- No severe distortion or clipping
- Minimal background noise
- Consistent volume levels
- Professional or semi-professional recording quality

### 8.2 Performance Standards

Acceptable performances must:
- Voice all characters in the screenplay
- Follow the screenplay's dialogue
- Complete the entire screenplay (not partial)
- Be delivered in a common audio format (MP3, WAV, M4A)

### 8.3 Turnaround Standards

- Performers must deliver within agreed deadline
- 7-day grace period automatically applies
- Beyond grace period, Writer may open dispute
- Consistent late delivery may affect Performer standing

---

## 9. Prohibited Conduct

### 9.1 For Writers

- Demanding unlimited revisions
- Requesting changes outside original scope without additional payment
- Threatening negative reviews to extract concessions
- Disputing after approving the final submission
- Using delivered audio without completing payment

### 9.2 For Performers

- Accepting commissions with no intent to deliver
- Delivering AI-generated audio without disclosure
- Plagiarizing other performances
- Demanding additional payment after acceptance
- Abandoning commissions without communication

### 9.3 Consequences

Violations may result in:
- Automatic loss of dispute
- Temporary or permanent platform suspension
- Forfeiture of pending payments
- Account termination

---

## 10. Platform Limitations

### 10.1 What the Platform Cannot Do

- Mediate personality disputes
- Enforce agreements made outside the platform
- Guarantee any specific outcome
- Provide legal advice
- Enforce copyright claims (must use legal system)

### 10.2 Binding Agreement

By using the commission feature, both parties agree:
- These rules constitute a binding agreement
- Platform decisions are final after appeal
- Disputes will not be pursued through other channels for amounts under $500
- Larger disputes may be subject to arbitration

---

## 11. Revision and Retake Policy

### 11.1 Included Retakes

- Retake count is set by Performer's pricing
- Typical range: 1-3 included retakes
- Retakes are for reasonable corrections only

### 11.2 Valid Retake Requests

- Mispronounced words
- Missed lines
- Audio quality issues
- Pacing adjustments
- Character voice adjustments (within reason)

### 11.3 Invalid Retake Requests

- Complete re-performance in different style
- Changes to the screenplay
- Requests that contradict previous feedback
- Unreasonable nitpicking

### 11.4 Additional Retakes

- May be purchased at Performer's retake rate
- Performer may decline additional retakes
- Writer may not dispute if declining additional retakes

---

## 12. Communication Requirements

### 12.1 Response Times

| Stage | Expected Response |
|-------|------------------|
| Initial Request | 72 hours |
| Accepted Commission | 48 hours |
| Revision Request | 48 hours |
| Dispute Response | 72 hours |

### 12.2 Unresponsive Parties

- 7+ days: Warning notification sent
- 14+ days: Other party may open dispute
- 21+ days: Automatic resolution in favor of responsive party

---

## 13. Special Circumstances

### 13.1 Medical/Family Emergencies

- Performer may request extension with documentation
- Writer must accommodate reasonable requests
- Pattern of emergencies may be reviewed

### 13.2 Technical Issues

- Platform outages do not count against deadlines
- Proof of technical issues must be provided
- Platform will verify reported issues

### 13.3 Force Majeure

- Natural disasters, wars, or other extraordinary events
- Both parties released from obligations
- Full refund to Writer
- No negative impact on Performer
